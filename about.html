About CannaShield

Your trusted partner in cannabis cybersecurity & compliance.

Our Story

CannaShield was born at the intersection of passion and protection.

Long before launching a cybersecurity firm, I dreamed of starting a cannabis dispensary, maybe even a grow shop or a lounge. Like many in this space, I was drawn to the culture, the plant, and the promise of a thriving, community-rooted industry.

But as I grew professionally in the world of cybersecurity and incident response, I saw a different opportunity — and a serious gap.

Every day, I watched small and midsize businesses fall victim to ransomware, phishing, and data breaches — not because they didn’t care, but because no one was speaking their language or offering solutions built for them.

That’s why I started CannaShield — to protect the people and businesses I care about. To bring trusted, practical, and affordable cybersecurity to dispensaries, growers, and cannabis entrepreneurs who deserve better than generic solutions or regulatory headaches.

Our Mission

To empower cannabis businesses with clear, cost-effective cybersecurity and compliance solutions tailored to their world — protecting people, data, and growth from the ground up.

Core Values

  1. Trust First: We build long-term relationships rooted in transparency, respect, and reliability.
  2. Built for Cannabis: Our solutions are designed specifically for cannabis operators — not retrofitted from other verticals.
  3. Keep It Practical: No fear-mongering. Just clear, actionable guidance that scales as you grow.
  4. Resilience Over Reaction: We focus on prevention, preparedness, and education.
  5. Rooted in Purpose: This isn’t just business. It’s personal. We’re here to protect a movement.

Our Vision

At CannaShield, our vision is to create a safer, more resilient cannabis industry.

We believe that every cannabis business, regardless of size, deserves access to affordable and effective cybersecurity solutions. By empowering operators with the tools and knowledge they need to protect their operations, we aim to foster trust, innovation, and growth in this rapidly evolving industry.

Our goal is to be more than a service provider — we strive to be a trusted partner in your journey, helping you navigate the complexities of compliance and security with confidence.
