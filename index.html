Cannabis Cybersecurity FAQ

What does CannaShield do?

CannaShield provides cybersecurity, GRC, vCISO, email security, training, and automation support for licensed cannabis businesses.

Who is CannaShield for?

CannaShield works with dispensaries, cultivators, manufacturers, processors, MSOs, and ancillary cannabis businesses that need practical cybersecurity and compliance support.

What does the free email security scorecard check?

The scorecard checks DMARC, SPF, DKIM, and related email authentication signals that influence whether attackers can spoof a business domain.

Does CannaShield replace an MSP?

No. CannaShield can work alongside an MSP by owning the cybersecurity program, compliance evidence, vendor risk, and audit-ready documentation that many MSPs do not manage.
