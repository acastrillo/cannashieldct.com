    FAQ

    Is this a real attack? Will it affect my systems?

    No. This tool performs passive, non-intrusive recon only — the same publicly available data an attacker would gather before ever touching your network. No systems are accessed, tested, or probed.

    What is a risk score of High or Critical?

    High (61–90) means active, exploitable exposure exists — usually an open management port or an active breach record. Critical (91+) means multiple critical findings are stacked. Either warrants immediate remediation.

    Why does my cannabis business need this?

    Ransomware actors run the same recon before targeting a dispensary or MSO. Knowing what they see lets you fix it first. License suspension and regulatory fines often follow a breach — the attack surface is where incidents start.

    What happens after I submit?
